# Device brand artwork

Logos identify device brands/platforms; no native CineScope apps or endorsement is implied. Trademarks belong to their respective owners. Files are hosted locally and preserve aspect ratios. Non-badge artwork is rendered monochrome with CSS.

- Apple, Xiaomi: Simple Icons 16.0.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0 library).
- Android, Samsung, LG, Sony, Apple TV: Simple Icons via https://cdn.simpleicons.org/, downloaded 2026-09-30 with `viewbox=auto`.
- Android robot: created and shared by Google; used according to Creative Commons Attribution 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. https://developer.android.com/distribute/marketing-tools/brand-guidelines
- Haier: https://haier.co.jp/wp-content/themes/haier-japan-theme/assets/images/logo-haier-blue.svg
- TCL: https://aws-obg-image-lb-3.tcl.com/content/dam/brandsite/it-resources/common/navigation/tcl-logo.svg
- Hisense: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hisense.svg (PD-textlogo), https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/47/Hisense.svg
- YaOS / YaOS X (TV Station platform): official support badges at https://alice.yandex.ru/support/ru/tv/, https://cdn-viewer.diplodoc.com/docs-assets/alice/rev/r21415268/ru/_assets/badges/ru/yaos.png and yaosx.png; unchanged.
- Computer: custom generic monitor SVG.
